## Slide 2: Problem
US hospital labs run 9 hours per analyst per week of manual compliance retesting.
38,000 labs are affected. Today they use spreadsheets and overnight couriers, which
fail because the new CLIA rule (2025) requires weekly rather than quarterly cycles.

## Slide 3: Why now
The CLIA update took effect in March 2025, cutting the retest window from 90 days
to 7. Labs cannot hire enough analysts to absorb a 12x increase in workload.

## Slide 4: Solution
Reverie schedules, runs, and files every retest automatically by connecting to the
LIS you already run. Labs cut retest labour by 78% in the first month.
